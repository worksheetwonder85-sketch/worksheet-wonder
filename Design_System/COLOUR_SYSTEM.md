# Worksheet Wonder: Colour System

## 1. Colour Philosophy
Worksheets should not look like a circus explosion of primary colors, as this is visually exhausting and distracting. The palette must be highly curated, using a sophisticated yet child-friendly scheme that looks as good in print as it does on a tablet.

## 2. Core Palette

### The Ink (Text & Borders)
*   **Dark Slate:** `#2C3E50`
*   *Usage:* All text, all SVG outlines, all panel borders. We never use pure black (`#000000`) as it is too harsh on bright white paper and causes eye strain. Slate provides maximum contrast with a softer, premium feel.

### The Paper (Backgrounds)
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary page background)
*   **Warm Cream:** `#FFFDF9` (Used for outer page matting or to warm up stark backgrounds).
*   **Scaffold Yellow:** `#FFFDE7` 
*   *Usage:* Very pale, almost pastel backgrounds used strictly inside specific instruction panels to draw attention without overpowering the main content.

### Brand & Interactive Colors
*   **Brand Orange (Primary Accent):** `#FF7043`
    *   *Usage:* Logos, critical starting dots on tracing activities, major call-to-actions.
*   **Success Green:** `#4CAF50` or `#00796B` (Darker for text)
    *   *Usage:* Positive reinforcement, "Great Job!" text, checkmarks.
*   **Playful Blue:** `#42A5F5` or `#48DBFB`
    *   *Usage:* Fun fact bubbles (`#E0F2F1` for light backgrounds), mascot accents.
*   **Grade Yellow:** `#FFE082`
    *   *Usage:* Meta badges (e.g., "Preschool", "Kindergarten").

## 3. Illustration Colors
When designing vocabulary SVGs, stick to easily identifiable, rich tones.
*   Reds: `#EF5350` (Apples, Ladybugs)
*   Browns: `#B27A4B`, `#8D6E63` (Bears, Trees, Dirt)
*   Yellows: `#FFCA28`, `#FFEB3B` (Sun, Bananas, Stars)
*   Greens: `#66BB6A` (Leaves, Frogs)

## 4. Print-Safe Considerations
*   All colors are chosen to map predictably to CMYK color spaces used by home inkjet printers.
*   When a user prints in "Black and White / Grayscale", the pastel backgrounds (`#FFFDE7`, `#E0F2F1`) will drop away almost entirely to pure white, saving ink. The Dark Slate (`#2C3E50`) will render as a crisp, solid 85% gray/black, ensuring text remains perfectly legible.
