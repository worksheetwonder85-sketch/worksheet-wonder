# Worksheet Wonder: Typography System

## 1. Core Font Families
Typography for 4–6 year olds must meet two strict criteria: it must be highly legible, and the letterforms must match the way children are taught to write (e.g., single-story 'a' and 'g').

*   **Primary Display Font:** `Fredoka`
    *   *Why:* It is a rounded, friendly, and chunky sans-serif. It feels inherently playful and approachable, mimicking the softness of children's toys and books. Used for Titles, Headings, and Badges.
*   **Secondary Body Font:** `Nunito`
    *   *Why:* Highly readable at smaller sizes with excellent letter spacing. Critically, its lowercase 'a' and 'g' are single-story (infant characters), matching standard handwriting curricula. Used for Instructions, Parent Notes, and general text.
*   **Handwriting Tracing Font:** Custom SVG paths or a specific tracing dashed font (e.g., `KG Primary Dots` or custom SVG).
    *   *Why:* Standard fonts do not provide the exact proportions required for proper stroke order and tracing mechanics.

## 2. Typographic Hierarchy & Sizing
*Sizes are specified in points (pt) for print accuracy.*

### Level 1: Hero Titles
*   **Font:** Fredoka, 700 (Bold)
*   **Size:** 26pt to 32pt
*   **Color:** Dark Slate (`#2C3E50`)
*   **Purpose:** The main worksheet title (e.g., "Letter B Discovery!"). It must be the first thing the eye registers.

### Level 2: Panel Headings & Brand Logo
*   **Font:** Fredoka, 700 (Bold)
*   **Size:** 12pt to 16pt
*   **Color:** Dark Slate (`#2C3E50`) or Brand Orange (`#FF7043`)
*   **Purpose:** Clear signposting for individual activities (e.g., "1. Drive along Letter Tracks").

### Level 3: Instructions & Learning Goals
*   **Font:** Nunito, 700 (Bold) for instructions, 400 (Italic) for goals
*   **Size:** 11pt to 12.5pt
*   **Color:** Dark Slate (`#2C3E50`)
*   **Purpose:** Large enough for early readers to attempt decoding, and clear enough for parents to read at a glance.

### Level 4: Parent Notes, Fun Facts, Footer
*   **Font:** Nunito, 400 (Regular) / Fredoka 700 (for tiny titles)
*   **Size:** 8.5pt to 9.5pt
*   **Color:** Muted Grey (`#555555` or `#7F8C8D`)
*   **Purpose:** Intentionally small to de-emphasize this content for the child, keeping their focus on the main activities, while remaining perfectly legible for adults.

### Level 5: Tracing / Handwriting Letters
*   **Size:** Massive. Equivalent to 60pt – 100pt depending on the section.
*   **Why:** 4–6 year olds do not have the fine motor control to write small. Letters must fill a 15mm–20mm vertical guideline height.
