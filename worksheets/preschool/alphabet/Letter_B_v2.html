Answer Key — Letter B

Activity 1 — Trace the Letters

Children should trace each letter following the numbered guides. Uppercase B: (1) straight line down, (2) top bump right, (3) bottom bump right. Lowercase b: (1) tall line down, (2) bump right at bottom.

Activity 2 — Circle Pictures That Begin with B

The child should circle:

  • Ball — begins with B
  • Butterfly — begins with B
  • Banana — begins with B
  • Bird — begins with B
  • Bee — begins with B

Do not circle: Cat, Apple, Dog.

Activity 3 — Circle Every Uppercase B

Letters to circle:

B   D   b   B   P   R   B   d   E   B   A   B   G   B

Total: 6 uppercase B’s. The lowercase b should not be circled.

Activity 4 — Match Picture to Beginning Letter

  • Bear → B
  • Apple → A
  • Banana → B
  • Bird → B

The letter C is the remaining unmatched distractor.

Activity 5 — Help the Bear Reach the Ball

The child should trace between the two dashed lane lines from left (Bear) to right (Ball). Any line staying within the lane boundaries is correct.

Activity 6 — Count the Objects

  • Balls: 3
  • Bananas: 5
  • Bees: 4

Activity 7 — Write the Letters

The child should independently write uppercase B (3 rows) and lowercase b (3 rows) on the lined paper. Accept any recognisable attempt. Focus on encouragement rather than perfection at this age.
