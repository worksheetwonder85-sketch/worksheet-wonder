Answer Key — Letter B

Activity 1 — Trace the Letters

Children should trace each letter following the directional arrows. Uppercase B starts with a straight line down, then two bumps to the right. Lowercase b starts with a tall line down, then one bump to the right at the bottom.

Activity 2 — Color Pictures That Begin with B

The child should color these pictures:

  • Ball — begins with B
  • Butterfly — begins with B
  • Banana — begins with B
  • Bird — begins with B
  • Bee — begins with B

These should not be colored: Cat, Apple, Dog.

Activity 3 — Circle Every Uppercase B

The letters to circle are:

B   D   b   B   P   R   B   d   E   B   A   B   G   B

Total: 6 uppercase B's should be circled. The lowercase b should not be circled.

Activity 4 — Match Picture to Beginning Letter

  • Bear → B
  • Apple → A
  • Banana → B

Activity 5 — Help the Bear Reach the Ball

The child should trace along the dotted winding path from the Bear on the left to the Ball on the right. Any line following the path is correct.

Activity 6 — Count the Objects

  • Balls: 3
  • Bananas: 5
  • Butterflies: 2
  • Bees: 4

Activity 7 — Write the Letters

The child should independently write uppercase B and lowercase b on the lined rows. Accept any recognizable attempt at the letter forms. Focus on encouragement rather than perfection at this age.
