## 1. Introduction & Strategic Vision

This blueprint defines the master curriculum matrix, activity library, and production framework for Worksheet Wonder. It maps all 28 core subject categories from Preschool through Grade 5 to transform our printable products into world-class, Montessori-aligned, commercially premium learning materials.

---

## 2. Global Curricular Architecture

Our curriculum is built on three core pillars:
1.  **Montessori-Inspired Isolation of Difficulty**: Each sheet targets exactly one sub-skill. We do not blend unrelated cognitive challenges (e.g., tracing letters while solving math problems).
2.  **Visual Frictionlessness**: Minimal, direct instructions. A child must understand the task within 3 seconds of looking at the page.
3.  **Grayscale-Optimized Aesthetic Design**: High-contrast, clean 0.8mm carbon black outlines, spacious margins (15mm top/bottom, 12mm sides), and a minimum of 40% empty white canvas.

### Category 1: Alphabet
*   **Curriculum Matrix**: Early literacy foundation focusing on letter shapes, name recognition, and case distinction.
*   **Difficulty Levels**: 
    *   *Level 1 (Foundation)*: Sensory finger tracing, uppercase visual shapes.
    *   *Level 2 (Developing)*: Lowercase shapes, case matching.
    *   *Level 3 (Mastery)*: Freehand letter formation, alphabet order.
*   **Worksheet Roadmap**: 26 individual letter pages + 10 alphabet sequencing games.
*   **Learning Objectives**: Students will trace, identify, and write uppercase and lowercase letters of the English alphabet.
*   **Activity Library**: Letter road tracks, circle-the-matching-case ladybug spot hunts, case match lines.
*   **Illustration Requirements**: Oversized letter track paths with direction arrows, friendly mascots waving beside tracer blocks.
*   **Bundle Structure**: `WW-PSC-ALPH-B01` (36 Pages).
*   **Worksheet Templates**: Preschool Standard (Wide tracing guidelines, large layout).
*   **Teacher/Parent Guides**: "Finger-trace before using pencils. Sound out name of the letter."
*   **Answer Keys**: Visual guides showing fully filled letters.
*   **Assessments**: "Trace the complete alphabet train path."

## 4. Production Roadmap & Verification System

To move this comprehensive curriculum matrix from catalog to publication, teams will follow the seven-step pipeline:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[1. Curriculum Check] --> B[2. Worksheet Plan]
    B --> C[3. SVG Illustration]
    C --> D[4. HTML Page Build]
    D --> E[5. Peer Review]
    E --> F[6. Edit & Polishing]
    F --> G[7. A4 PDF Export]
```

### Peer Review Quality Checklist
- **Whitespace**: Is at least 40% of the page blank to prevent cognitive fatigue?
- **Grayscale Contrast**: Do adjacent fills have at least a 30% difference in grayscale density?
- **Isolation of Challenge**: Is the worksheet free of mixed skills (e.g., no letter writing drills inside math tables)?
- **A4 Safety Insets**: Do borders sit within the $15\text{mm}$ top/bottom and $12\text{mm}$ side printer-safe margins?
