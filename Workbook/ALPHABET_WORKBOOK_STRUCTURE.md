# 🔤 Alphabet Workbook Complete Structure (Letters A–Z)

This document establishes the exact educational, visual, and architectural specification for every letter page (A to Z) in **"My First Alphabet Workbook"**.

## Master Letter Matrix (A to Z Specifications)

### Letter A
- **Learning Objectives**: Identify uppercase A and lowercase a; recognize initial /æ/ sound; form strokes correctly.
- **Target Vocabulary**: Apple, Ant, Alligator.
- **Skills**: Letter recognition, phonemic awareness, fine motor tracing, visual discrimination, independent writing.
- **Activities**: Panels 1–8 (Meet A, Trace A, Trace a, Circle A/a, Beginning Sounds, Find 5 A's, Write A/a, Reward).
- **Illustrations**: Apple (Red fruit with green leaf), Ant (Black 3-segment body with antennae), Alligator (Green smiling alligator).
- **Difficulty**: Level 1 (Gentle introduction).
- **Estimated Time**: 10–15 minutes.
- **Answer Key**: Red banner; Red circles on A/a cells (R1C2, R1C4, R1C6, R2C1, R2C3, R2C5, R3C2, R3C4, R3C6, R4C1, R4C3, R4C5, R5C2, R5C4, R5C6); Green checkmarks on Apple, Ant, Alligator; 5 hidden A's circled.
- **Teacher Tip**: Emphasize short /æ/ sound as in "Apple". Practice air-writing diagonal strokes.
- **Parent Tip**: Point out real apples and ants during snack time and outdoor play.
- **Required SVG Assets**: `apple.svg`, `ant.svg`, `alligator.svg`, `star_reward.svg`.
- **Required Components**: `header_badge`, `user_bar`, `title_row`, `activity_panel_1_to_8`, `footer_brand`.
- **SEO Keywords**: letter a worksheet, trace letter a, kindergarten letter a, free printable alphabet worksheet a.

### Letter Y
- **Learning Objectives**: Identify uppercase Y and lowercase y; recognize initial /j/ sound; handle descending slant tail.
- **Target Vocabulary**: Yak, Yo-yo, Yacht.
- **Skills**: V-junction stem, descending tail slant below baseline.
- **Activities**: Panels 1–8 (Meet Y, Trace Y, Trace y, Circle Y/y, Beginning Sounds, Find 5 Y's, Write Y/y, Reward).
- **Illustrations**: Yak (Hairy brown Tibetan yak), Yo-yo (Red spinning yo-yo), Yacht (White sailboat yacht).
- **Difficulty**: Level 2 (Descending tail stroke).
- **Estimated Time**: 10–15 minutes.
- **Answer Key**: Red banner; Red circles on Y/y cells; Green checkmarks on Yak, Yo-yo, Yacht; 5 hidden Y's circled.
- **Teacher Tip**: Lowercase y has a short slant right, and a LONG slant left going below the line.
- **Parent Tip**: Play with a yo-yo or watch sailboat videos while saying /j/ /j/ yo-yo!
- **Required SVG Assets**: `yak.svg`, `yoyo.svg`, `yacht.svg`.
- **Required Components**: Standard 8-Panel Matrix.
- **SEO Keywords**: letter y worksheet, trace letter y, y sound activities, yak yoyo yacht.

### Letter Z
- **Learning Objectives**: Identify uppercase Z and lowercase z; recognize buzz /z/ sound; execute zig-zag horizontal & diagonal strokes.
- **Target Vocabulary**: Zebra, Zipper, Zoo.
- **Skills**: Top horizontal line, diagonal backslant, bottom horizontal line.
- **Activities**: Panels 1–8 (Meet Z, Trace Z, Trace z, Circle Z/z, Beginning Sounds, Find 5 Z's, Write Z/z, Reward).
- **Illustrations**: Zebra (Striped black & white zebra), Zipper (Jacket zipper), Zoo (Zoo gate entrance with animals).
- **Difficulty**: Level 2 (Zig-zag direction changes).
- **Estimated Time**: 10–15 minutes.
- **Answer Key**: Red banner; Red circles on Z/z cells; Green checkmarks on Zebra, Zipper, Zoo; 5 hidden Z's circled.
- **Teacher Tip**: "Slide right, slant down left, slide right again!".
- **Parent Tip**: Zip up jackets while making a loud buzzing /zzzzz/ sound!
- **Required SVG Assets**: `zebra.svg`, `zipper.svg`, `zoo.svg`.
- **Required Components**: Standard 8-Panel Matrix.
- **SEO Keywords**: letter z worksheet, trace letter z, z sound activities, zebra zipper zoo.
