## 1. Grade-by-Grade Curriculum Progression

### Preschool (Age 3-4 years)
- **Fine Motor Skills**: Line tracing (straight, zig-zag, curved), shape tracing, scissor safety cutouts, coloring inside boundaries.
- **Phonics & Alphabet**: Upper & lowercase letter recognition A-Z, letter tracing, initial phoneme sounds.
- **Early Maths**: Number counting 1-10, big vs small comparisons, basic 2D shape sorting.
- **Art & Craft**: Primary color identification, pattern completion, finger-painting outlines.

### Kindergarten (Age 4-6 years)
- **Mathematics**: Counting objects 1-20, single-digit addition (sums to 10), single-digit subtraction, 2D/3D shapes, AB/AABB patterns.
- **English & Phonics**: CVC word family blends (cat, dog, pig), sight words Level 1, rhyming word pairs, decodable sentence reading.
- **EVS & Science**: My body parts, five senses, living vs non-living things, weather and seasons.
- **Logical Reasoning**: Shadow matching, odd one out, 4-step story sequencing cards.

### Grade 1 (Age 6-7 years)
- **Mathematics**: Numbers up to 100, two-digit addition (no regrouping), basic subtraction, place value (tens and ones), reading analog clocks (hours).
- **English & Grammar**: Nouns and pronouns, action verbs, capitalization rules, sentence punctuation, short reading passages.
- **Science & Environment**: Animal habitats, food chains, states of matter (solid/liquid/gas), solar system intro.
- **Computer Skills**: Computer parts identification, keyboard layout, mouse practice, safe digital habits.

### Grade 2 (Age 7-8 years)
- **Mathematics**: Two-digit addition/subtraction with regrouping, multiplication tables (2, 5, 10), introduction to fractions (1/2, 1/4), coin money counting.
- **English & Writing**: Adjectives and adverbs, conjunctions, paragraph writing with word banks, vocabulary expansion.
- **Science**: Human skeletal system, plant life cycles, weather patterns, simple machines (lever, pulley).

### Grade 3 (Age 8-9 years)
- **Mathematics**: 3-digit and 4-digit numbers, multiplication facts up to 12x12, basic division, equivalent fractions, perimeter and area.
- **English**: Subject-verb agreement, synonyms and antonyms, creative story prompts, comprehension inference.
- **Science & Social Studies**: Forces and motion, ecosystems, world geography maps, solar system planets.

### Grade 4 (Age 9-10 years)
- **Mathematics**: Long multiplication and division, decimals and percentages, angles and geometry, data bar graphs.
- **English**: Direct and indirect speech, essay structure, prefixes and suffixes, multi-paragraph comprehension.
- **Science & STEAM**: Electricity and simple circuits, human digestive system, energy transformations, STEM building blueprints.

### Grade 5 (Age 10-11 years)
- **Mathematics**: Fraction arithmetic (+, -, x, /), introduction to algebraic expressions, volume and surface area, ratios and rates.
- **English**: Complex sentences, persuasive essay writing, poetry analysis, Greek/Latin root words.
- **Science & Computer**: Cell structures, physical vs chemical changes, introductory algorithm logic & Scratch coding principles.
