# 🔤 Worksheet Wonder Typography Guide

This document defines typography choices, Google Font integrations, point scales, stroke weights, and letter spacing.

---

## 1. Approved Font Families
- **Display & Title Font**: `'Fredoka', sans-serif` (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700)
- **Body & Instruction Font**: `'Nunito', sans-serif` (Google Fonts, weights 600, 700, 800)

---

## 3. Letter Spacing & Line Height Rules
- **Tracing Row Spacing**: Tracing letters spaced with `letter-spacing: 5mm` to provide ample room for little fingers.
- **Handwriting Baseline**: `height: 12mm` per baseline with dashed midline positioned at exact 50% height.
