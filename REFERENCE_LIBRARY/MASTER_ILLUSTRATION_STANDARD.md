# Master Illustration Standard

## Principles
- All illustrations must be resolution-independent SVG vector graphic paths.
- Friendly, encouraging aesthetic tailored for young children (soft curves, warm faces).
- Clean line weights (1.5px to 3px) for crisp printing on monochrome or color printers.

## Palette Standards
- Primary Outlines: #2C3E50 or #333333
- Soft Fills: HSL pastel tones with subtle gradients.
- Highlight Dots: White semi-transparent overlays (opacity="0.4").
