# Master Research Guide

## Overview
This document establishes the research protocol for analyzing educational standards, competitive materials, and pedagogical frameworks for Preschool through Grade 5.

## Research Objectives
1. Pedagogical Integrity: Ensure alignment with EYFS, Common Core State Standards (CCSS), and ACARA framework.
2. Age-Appropriate Learning: Match cognitive developmental stages (Piaget/Vygotsky) for ages 3 to 11.
3. Visual & Structural Benchmarking: Evaluate layout clarity, whitespace utilization, font legibility, and illustration effectiveness.

## Research Protocol
- Analyze target learning benchmarks per grade/subject.
- Deconstruct reference worksheets into core activities.
- Identify strengths, weaknesses, and optimization opportunities for Worksheet Wonder.
- Document findings in the standardized MASTER_ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md.
