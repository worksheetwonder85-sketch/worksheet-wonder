# 🎨 Worksheet Wonder Illustration Style Guide

This document defines vector illustration art direction, character anatomy, line weights, facial feature rules, and HSL fills.

---

## 1. Character Anatomy & Style Principles
- **Memorable & Friendly**: Every illustration is a character with a personality, expressive eyes, and a cheerful smile.
- **Rounded Organic Geometry**: Curved, friendly silhouettes (`rx`, `ry`, smooth bezier curves); no sharp scary corners.
- **Line Weight Uniformity**: Main outlines drawn with clean `2.0px` stroke in Warm Charcoal (`#37474F`).
- **Glinting Eyes**: Expressive white highlight dots (`fill="white"`) in pupils to bring characters to life.
- **Soft Highlights**: Semi-transparent white highlight overlays (`fill="white" opacity="0.3"`) adding 3D depth without raster graphics.

## 2. Character Specifications for Letter A

### 🍎 Apple Character ("Annie Apple")
- **Body**: Bright cherry red (`#FF5252`) rounded apple body.
- **Face**: Big cheerful white eyes with dark pupils (`#37474F`) + glint dots, cute smiling mouth (`#C62828`), rosy pink cheeks (`#FF8A80`).
- **Details**: Brown wooden stem (`#5D4037`), vibrant green leaf (`#4CAF50`) with leaf vein, soft glossy highlight curve.

### 🐜 Ant Character ("Andy Ant")
- **Body**: 3-segment friendly ant body in deep navy-grey (`#37474F` & `#455A64`).
- **Head**: Round head with large white cartoon eyes + pupils looking up happily, cute antennae with ball tips (`r="2"`).
- **Legs**: 6 little shoes/boots (`#FF7043`) on legs.

### 🐊 Alligator Character ("Albie Alligator")
- **Body**: Bright emerald green (`#4CAF50`) friendly alligator profile.
- **Face**: Waving claw hand, big smiling mouth with cute white triangle teeth, cheerful eye ridge.
- **Details**: Soft yellow belly (`#FFF59D`), back scutes/spikes (`#388E3C`).
