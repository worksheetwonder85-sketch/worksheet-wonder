## 3. Print-Safety & Contrast Standards
- **Monochrome Print Safe**: All primary text (`#37474F`) and card borders maintain minimum `7:1` contrast ratio against card backgrounds.
- **Grayscale Conversion**: Card backgrounds print as soft 3% light grey, maintaining 100% legibility on black-and-white laser printers.
- **No Heavy Black Lines**: Replaced `#000000` and `#2C3E50` with warm `#37474F` to eliminate harsh contrast and save printer ink.
