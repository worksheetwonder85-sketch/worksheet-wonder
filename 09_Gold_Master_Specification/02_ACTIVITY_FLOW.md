# 02. Activity Flow Specification

Every worksheet from A–Z must strictly follow this exact 11-step learning sequence. Deviation is prohibited.

### 1. Header
*   **Purpose:** Branding and student identification.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 180 mm (W) x 15 mm (H).
*   **Content:** Logo (Left), "Name: _______" (Right, 60mm rule).
*   **Child Objective:** Learn to write their own name.

### 2. Letter Introduction
*   **Purpose:** Visual anchor identifying the worksheet's target letter.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 90 mm (W) x 65 mm (H).
*   **Text Size:** 142 pt (Uppercase & Lowercase pair).
*   **Teacher Objective:** Introduce the visual shape of the letter.

### 3. Stroke Order Demonstration
*   **Purpose:** Teach correct starting points and directional paths.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 40 mm (W) x 45 mm (H) per letter.
*   **Interaction:** Visual reference only. Arrow width 1.5 mm. Number size 12 pt.

### 4. Finger Tracing
*   **Purpose:** Tactile memory mapping without the pressure of a pencil.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 80 mm (W) x 65 mm (H).
*   **Interaction:** "Road" style lettering with dashed midlines. Child uses index finger.
*   **Child Objective:** Build gross motor pathways before attempting fine motor control.

### 5. Vocabulary
*   **Purpose:** Phonics association and word recognition.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 45 mm (W) x 65 mm (H) per vocabulary card (3 cards total).
*   **Illustration Size:** 40 mm x 40 mm.
*   **Text Size:** 16 pt Nunito.

### 6. Colour Illustrations
*   **Purpose:** Visual engagement and context.
*   **Interaction:** High-contrast SVG vectors placed next to vocabulary words.

### 7. Colouring Activity
*   **Purpose:** Hand-eye coordination and creative break.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 90 mm (W) x 90 mm (H).
*   **Interaction:** Child colors an outline SVG (e.g., Bear_outline.svg).
*   **Teacher Objective:** Observe pencil grip and pressure.

### 8. Letter Tracing
*   **Purpose:** Guided fine-motor control.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 180 mm (W) x 18 mm (H) per row. (2 Rows).
*   **Interaction:** Child traces dotted letters over a 3-line educational guide.

### 9. Independent Writing
*   **Purpose:** Testing spatial awareness and memory recall.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 180 mm (W) x 18 mm (H) per row. (1 Row).
*   **Interaction:** Child writes the letter starting from a single dot, with no tracing guides.

### 10. Mini Review
*   **Purpose:** Comprehension check.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 180 mm (W) x 25 mm (H).
*   **Interaction:** Simple cognitive task (e.g., "Circle the apples with the letter A").

### 11. Encouragement Footer
*   **Purpose:** Positive reinforcement and curriculum mapping.
*   **Exact Dimensions:** 180 mm (W) x 10 mm (H).
*   **Text Size:** 9 pt.
*   **Content:** Star rating checkboxes, Teacher/Parent Tips.
