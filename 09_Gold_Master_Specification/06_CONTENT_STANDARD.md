# 06. Content Standard Specification

This document governs the quality, accuracy, and pedagogical logic of the JSON database injecting content into the Worksheet Wonder layouts.

## 1. Vocabulary Selection
*   **Phonetic Accuracy:** Vocabulary words must represent the most common phonetic sound of the letter (e.g., 'Apple' for /a/, not 'Acorn'; 'Igloo' for /i/, not 'Ice').
*   **Syllable Limit:** Primary vocabulary (the 3 Hero words) should be 1–2 syllables maximum.
*   **Cognitive Recognition:** Words must represent concrete, highly recognizable nouns. Avoid abstract concepts, verbs, or visually ambiguous items (e.g., 'Happiness', 'Run', 'Amorphous').

## 2. Teacher & Parent Tips
*   **Length:** Maximum 85 characters. Must fit on one single line in the 11pt footer.
*   **Tone:** Encouraging, actionable, non-academic jargon. 
    *   *Good:* "Ask your child to find 'B' words in the kitchen!"
    *   *Bad:* "Assess the child's phonemic awareness via environmental print."

## 3. Curriculum Mapping
Every JSON payload must contain tags aligning with major early years frameworks.
*   **EYFS (UK):** Early Years Foundation Stage mapping (e.g., Literacy - Writing).
*   **CCSS (US):** Common Core State Standards (e.g., RF.K.1.D - Recognize and name all uppercase and lowercase letters of the alphabet).
*   **Objectives:** Must explicitly state Fine Motor Objective (e.g., Pencil grip) and Cognitive Objective (e.g., Phonics recognition).

## 4. Inclusivity & Representation
*   Illustrations depicting people must include a diverse range of skin tones, hair textures, and abilities.
*   Gender-neutral terminology should be favored in teacher/parent tips.
